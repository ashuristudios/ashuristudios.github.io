Eastbound: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026. Applies to the Android game Eastbound: Train Yard Puzzle, made by Ashuri ("we", "us").

The short version

  • You do not need an account. We never ask for your email, phone number, real name, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • If you use the daily league, a small public profile (a nickname you pick, an avatar, a score and your country) is stored online so other players can see it.
  • Ads, when shown, are served by Google AdMob. You can change your ad choices in Settings.

1. What stays on your phone

Your progress (yards cleared, stars, coins, hints, stamps, badges, settings, language, nickname and avatar choices) is saved in the game's private storage on your device. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. What is sent to our servers (the daily league)

The league uses Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, servers in Europe, region europe-west1). When you finish a yard with the league on, the game sends:

DataWhy
An anonymous player id created by Firebase (a random string, not linked to your Google account)To recognise your entry and keep one entry per player
Your nickname, avatar, frame and badge countsTo show you in the league standings
Your score for the day and the time of the last updateTo rank you
Your country (two-letter code)To show a flag next to your name. You can hide it in your profile.
A report, if you report another player: the reported player's id and the reasonTo review abuse. Blocking a player is stored only on your phone.

This profile is visible to the other players in your league group. Do not put personal information in your nickname.

Country lookup. To work out your country, the game asks a free Cloudflare service (cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace). Cloudflare necessarily sees your IP address when you make that request; we do not store your IP address. If the lookup fails, the game uses the region setting of your phone instead.

Retention. League entries are tied to the daily round and are removed or become unreachable after the round ends; we may delete old rounds at any time. Reports are kept as long as needed to deal with abuse.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game may show ads: optional rewarded videos that you choose to watch for a hint or extra coins, and short full-screen ads between yards once you have played for a while (a paid option to remove these may be offered). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising id, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud and, if you agree, to personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, the game asks for your consent first through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or turn off your advertising id in your Android settings. See how Google uses data in ads.

4. Purchases

Purchases, if offered, are handled by Google Play. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, never your payment details.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use your data to profile you ourselves. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13; the game is not directed at them. If you believe a child has used the league, contact us and we will delete the entry.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show or delete the league data linked to you, and, if you are in the EU/EEA or UK, to exercise your GDPR rights (access, correction, deletion, objection, complaint to your data protection authority). Email us with your nickname and approximate score, because we cannot otherwise tell which anonymous id is yours. Local data on your phone is under your control and is removed by uninstalling the game.

7. Security

Data is transferred over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Google Cloud. No online service is perfectly secure, so we collect as little as possible.

8. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and if the change is important we will tell you in the game.
